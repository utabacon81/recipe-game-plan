Recipe

Ingredients

One per line. Prep after a comma (diced, minced, cubed) becomes a prep task. Lines ending in a colon, like For the topping:, are skipped.

Steps

One step per line. To set a time, end the line with 12m, 1h or 1h30m. Add wait for hands-off time: Simmer covered 1h30m wait. Hands-on time grows with batch size, wait time doesn't. With no tag, the time is estimated from the text.
